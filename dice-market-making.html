Dice Market-Making Agent

Everesteer Trading Challenge · Python / NumPy · Team lead, two-person team

As team lead, I developed the Bayesian inference and simulation core, built the evaluation framework, integrated components and made deployment decisions for an adaptive market-making agent. The approach combined opponent modelling with Monte Carlo quote selection in a simulated dice market.

Final challenge standing: 27th of 106 candidates (Elo 1379).

A detailed technical write-up is being held pending clarification of the organisers’ publication conditions.

This project concerns a simulated dice game. Challenge performance does not represent financial-market returns.
